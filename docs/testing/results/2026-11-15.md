## Problems found

### 1. Samples inflated by time away

A sample is the gap between a death and the next time the player sees a fresh mob of that npcID near the spot. If the player leaves and comes back, the time away is counted as respawn time.

| Time | Mob | Sample | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11:30:08 | Beastmaster (784) | 9m 24s | after a gap in activity 11:23 to 11:29 |
| 11:54:37 | Berserker (783) | 16m 45s | no log lines 11:40 to 11:54 (player away) |
| 11:56:30 | Headhunter (781) | 16m 00s | same gap |
| 12:03:12 | Scout (782) | 49m 23s | death at 11:13, area revisited much later |
| 12:22:56 | Mystic (780) | 15m 09s | player fighting about 90 yd away in between |
| 12:37:23 | Scout (782) | 22m 54s | |
| 12:40:00 | Mystic (780) | 16m 53s | |

Proposed fix: record when the player is within `MATCH_RADIUS` of each pending death. Drop the sample (or treat it as an upper bound only) if the player was out of range for part of the gap.

### 2. Suspiciously short samples (wrong match)

| Time | Mob | Sample |
|---|---|---|
| 11:14:37 | Beastmaster (784) | 1m 36s |
| 11:30:17 | Scout (782) | 1m 08s |
| 12:33:39 | Scout (782) | 1m 06s |

Scout area #1 ended with [68, 260, 287, 66]: two of four samples are far below the rest. Likely cause: a different mob of the same npcID that was alive the whole time came into nameplate range near the death spot. Any never-seen GUID counts as a respawn.

Proposed fix: require stronger proof for short gaps, for example the player was already standing near the spot when the new GUID appeared (it popped in, rather than came into view).

### 3. NPC pets counted as spawns

Skullsplitter Panther (756) is a pet summoned by Skullsplitter Hunter (669) and Beastmaster (784). Its deaths line up with its owner's (for example 11:38:39 Hunter and Panther in the same second). All five Panther samples (2m 50s, 58s, 9m 43s, 15m 54s, 3m 05s) are really owner respawns and should be dropped.

Verified in game: the Panther tooltip shows a "Skullsplitter Hunter's Pet" line.

Proposed fix: in the tooltip hook, detect the pet line by matching the client's `UNITNAME_TITLE_PET` format string (not hardcoded English). Mark the npcID as a pet permanently and delete its samples. Pets record no deaths, learn nothing and show no timer.

### 4. Most respawns are never learned

149 deaths produced only 24 samples. Most fresh mobs were first seen in combat (pulled before mouseover or target), so they went to the add filter. This is by design, but it means a large share of real respawns teach nothing.

Proposed fix: when a mob is first seen in combat and a death of the same npcID nearby is pending, keep the gap as an upper bound ("respawns in at most X") instead of discarding it.

### 5. Totems logged as creatures

Healing Ward (3560) and Earthgrab Totem (6066) show up as `add` and `death` lines. Harmless now, but they would add noise to a zone average. Should be filtered by `UnitClassification` (`trivial` / `minus`) or the pet check. Their classification is not verified yet.

## Planned feature: zone average

Decided during the session:

- Group samples by zone to give an average respawn time, used for mobs with no data of their own.
- Only `UnitClassification == "normal"` mobs feed it. Rares, rare elites, elites and world bosses keep only their own timers.
- Quest mobs are detected by quest lines in the tooltip (quest title / objective line types) and keep their own timers. Chosen over position-based checks because quest mobs wander. Known limit: quest lines only show while the player has the quest.
- Pets are excluded (see problem 3).
- Still open: fallback only or always shown, zone or subzone, whether the quest flag is permanent per npcID.

## Fix list (priority order)

1. Drop samples when the player was out of range during the gap (problem 1).
2. Tighten the new-GUID match for short gaps (problem 2).
3. Automatic pet detection from the tooltip pet line (problem 3).
4. Keep in-combat first sightings as upper bounds (problem 4).
5. Zone average, normal mobs only, quest mobs flagged by tooltip.
