## Problems

1. **No learning while roaming a big camp.** Every respawn that matched in the Wastewander and Gordunni camps was dropped as away. With AWAY_RADIUS 100 and a budget of 0.2 * gap (about 25 s for a 2 min respawn), moving through a 450 to 500 yd camp passes the limit. Fixed in PR #5 (200 yd, min(120, 0.5 * gap)); needs a session with that code loaded.
2. **Most deaths never match at all.** In the two big camps, 59 deaths gave only 7 matches. The respawn has to be seen within 60 yd of where the player stood at the death, and the player has moved on by then. Left as is for now (wider radius brings back false matches in dense camps); revisit with PR #5 data.
3. **Zone pool is empty.** Every mob that gave a sample tonight was quest-flagged (Southsea, Wastewander), so nothing fed the zone average. While questing, most grind targets are quest mobs, so the zone fallback may rarely fill. Design question for later.
